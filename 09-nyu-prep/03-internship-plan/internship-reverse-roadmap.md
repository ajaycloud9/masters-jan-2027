## 1. The answer in one screen

**Target:** a full-time, 10–12 week marketing-analytics internship in New York, **June–August 2028**, authorised by CPT through the internship course MASY1-GC 3910, and used as your final 3 credits.

**Why not Summer 2027?** You cannot legally take a U.S. internship then. CPT requires *two prior consecutive full-time semesters* for graduate students unless the programme mandates an internship in year one, which MSMA does not [5][8]. Spring 2027 + Fall 2027 completes that in December 2027. The internship course also has GPA and credit requirements [2]; your earlier handbook records these as 18 credits and a 3.0 GPA.

**What it takes:** 36 credits in four registered terms, with the 3910 internship as the last course.
